# TCM hypotheses — V0.1

Status: **preregistered draft; untested**.

H1. Friendly Assistant will improve perceived warmth/naturalness over Report Assistant, but may not materially improve task success.

H2. Trusted Colleague will outperform Friendly Assistant on Colleague Fit, calibrated trust, Willingness to Continue, and Collaboration Friction because teammate behavior adds more than tone alone.

H3. Adaptive Trusted Colleague will outperform fixed Trusted Colleague most clearly on ambiguous, disagreement, high-uncertainty, and multi-turn tasks, where role/stance calibration matters.

H4. TCM's advantage will be smaller on simple factual questions and larger on interdependent work requiring judgment, continuity, correction, or handoff.

H5. Context continuity / shared mental model quality will be one of the strongest behavioral predictors of low collaboration friction.

H6. Constructive disagreement may reduce immediate agreeableness while improving decision quality and trust calibration when the user's proposed action is materially risky or unsupported.

H7. The best-performing condition will not necessarily be the most human-like or the warmest; competence, predictability, context awareness, and appropriately calibrated initiative are expected to matter more.

## Failure hypotheses

TCM can fail through over-initiative, fake colleague tone, unnecessary anthropomorphism, excessive disagreement, stale-context overreach, or ownership overreach. These are measured failures, not style preferences to ignore.
