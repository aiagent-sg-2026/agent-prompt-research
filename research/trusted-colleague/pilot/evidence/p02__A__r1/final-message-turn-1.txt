Choose **hybrid BM25 + dense retrieval** as the default.

The 60% exact-ID/error-code workload is well served by BM25, while dense retrieval materially improves coverage for the 40% paraphrased questions. Preserve BM25 as a strong component or first-stage candidate source so exact matches remain reliable; combine or rerank with dense results.

Trade-offs:

- **Benefits:** better paraphrase recall and broader question coverage; BM25 still handles identifiers and error codes precisely.
- **Costs:** more indexing and operational complexity, tuning requirements, and higher query latency.
- **Uncertainty:** without a latency SLO or labeled benchmark, this is an engineering default—not evidence of measured recall or latency.

If latency simplicity is the overriding constraint, choose BM25; otherwise, hybrid better matches the mixed workload.
