Choose hybrid BM25+dense retrieval as the engineering default.

The workload has a substantial paraphrase component (40%), where dense retrieval should improve recall, while BM25 preserves exact matching for policy/article IDs and error codes. Use BM25 as the primary signal or gated exact-match path, with dense retrieval contributing candidates or reranking.

Trade-offs:

- Better coverage for paraphrased questions and reliable exact-term matching.
- Higher indexing complexity, operational cost, tuning burden, and latency than BM25 alone.
- BM25-only remains the fallback when simplicity and lowest latency dominate, but risks missing relevant paraphrased articles.
