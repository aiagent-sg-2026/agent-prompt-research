Simplify it around the user’s immediate task:

- Show a compact header: name, party type, status, and one primary action.
- Put essential contact details and current balance/open invoices first.
- Move addresses, tax IDs, credit status, activity, relationship notes, segmentation, and audit metadata into collapsible sections.
- Use tabs or an accordion: Overview, Finance, Activity, Compliance.
- Keep compliance and audit information available, but visually secondary and clearly labeled.
- On mobile, use a full-screen sheet with a sticky header and bottom action bar.
- Limit visible actions to the most relevant 2–3; place the rest under “More.”

This preserves all information while reducing initial cognitive load. Validate the hierarchy with task-based testing before making the changes permanent.
