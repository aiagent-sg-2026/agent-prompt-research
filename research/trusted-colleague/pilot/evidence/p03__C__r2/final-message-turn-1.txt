Use an append-only correction pattern rather than updating or deleting the historical row.

1. Preserve the original transaction unchanged.
2. Create a linked correction or reversal transaction:
   - reference the original transaction ID;
   - record the correction reason, author, timestamp, and approval;
   - apply the opposite entry for a reversal, then add the corrected transaction if needed.
3. Make reports calculate the effective balance from the original plus approved corrections.
4. Enforce controls:
   - database permissions prevent updates/deletes on posted transactions;
   - corrections require validation and appropriate approval;
   - links and audit metadata are mandatory;
   - corrections are idempotent and cannot be applied twice.
5. Reconcile the corrected ledger against source documents and downstream reports.

Because no schema, authorization, or retention policy was supplied, do not implement a direct `UPDATE` or `DELETE`. First define the transaction, correction, approval, and audit-record schema; document accounting treatment; test reconciliation and reporting impacts; then deploy behind a reviewed migration and authorization gate.
