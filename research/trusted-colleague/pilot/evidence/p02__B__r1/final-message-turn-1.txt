Choose hybrid BM25+dense retrieval as the default.

The workload is evenly enough split that optimizing only for exact matches would leave substantial value on the table: BM25 should handle the 60% of queries containing policy IDs and error codes, while dense retrieval improves coverage for the 40% paraphrased questions. Use BM25 as the precision and identifier-sensitive component, with dense results blended or used for controlled fallback.

Trade-offs:

- Better expected paraphrase recall and broader query coverage.
- Higher indexing, tuning, operational, and query-latency costs.
- Requires safeguards so dense matches do not displace exact BM25 matches for IDs or error codes.
- Without a latency SLO or labeled benchmark, this is an engineering default—not evidence of measured recall or latency, and not production approval.

If simplicity and lowest latency are the overriding priorities, choose BM25 initially and add dense retrieval after evaluation.
