Simplify it around the most common decision: “Who is this, and what needs attention?”

Proposed modal:

- Header: name, type, status, and primary action
- Key facts: phone/email, location, account owner, and risk or credit status
- Attention needed: overdue invoices, blocked issues, or urgent activity
- Recent activity: show the latest 3 items
- Actions: contact, edit, create invoice, and more
- Secondary sections: relationship notes, segmentation, tax IDs, and audit history collapsed under “Details”

On mobile, use a single-column layout with expandable sections. Keep compliance and audit information available, but move it out of the default view rather than removing it. Could you clarify which action users perform most often so the header and primary action can be prioritized around it?
