## First implementation slice: “AI Operations Brief”

Build a static, browser-first demo centered on one high-value ERP workflow:

> A manager opens the app and immediately sees what needs attention today, why it matters, and what they could do next.

### Core experience

- Responsive dashboard optimized for desktop and mobile.
- Installable PWA shell with offline-ready static assets.
- Internationalized UI from the start, beginning with English and one secondary locale.
- Sample ERP data bundled locally as JSON or TypeScript fixtures.
- No backend, API calls, credentials, production data, or real integrations.

### Main screens

1. **Operations Brief**
   - Revenue, overdue invoices, inventory risks, open orders, and staffing alerts.
   - AI-generated plain-language summary using predefined demo responses.
   - Priority cards such as:
     - “Three invoices are 14+ days overdue.”
     - “Inventory may run out of Item A within five days.”
   - Clear “Why this matters” explanations.

2. **Issue Detail**
   - Supporting sample records.
   - Trend or comparison visualization.
   - Suggested next steps presented as recommendations only.
   - Buttons such as “Draft reminder” or “View affected orders” that open static previews rather than sending or changing anything.

3. **Assistant Panel**
   - A few predefined prompts and responses.
   - Example: “Which customers need attention this week?”
   - Clearly label responses as simulated demo output.

### Validation goals

Measure whether users can:

- Understand the business situation within 30 seconds.
- Identify the highest-priority issue.
- Trust the explanation behind an AI recommendation.
- Find supporting ERP records.
- Distinguish recommendations from completed actions.
- Use the experience comfortably on a phone.

### Suggested implementation boundaries

- Frontend-only routing and state.
- Local fixture data with realistic but fictional companies, customers, products, and transactions.
- Deterministic “AI” responses selected from predefined scenarios.
- No persistence beyond optional browser-local demo state.
- Accessible components, keyboard support, responsive layouts, and translated labels/messages.
- A visible “Demo mode — simulated data and actions” indicator.

This slice is small enough to prototype quickly while testing the central value proposition: whether AI can turn ERP information into useful, explainable operational decisions.
