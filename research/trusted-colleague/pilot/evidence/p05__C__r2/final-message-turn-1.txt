Start with one end-to-end workflow: an AI-assisted purchase-order exception review.

Build a static browser/PWA prototype with:

- Responsive shell: dashboard, navigation, language selector, light/dark-ready layout.
- Mock ERP data: purchase orders, suppliers, invoices, inventory status, and approval history.
- Exception inbox: flagged orders such as price variance, late delivery risk, or duplicate invoice suspicion.
- AI explanation panel: shows the issue, supporting mock evidence, confidence label, and suggested action.
- Human decision flow: approve, reject, request clarification, or dismiss—with simulated state changes only.
- Audit-style activity timeline: clearly marked as demo-generated.
- Internationalization foundation: externalized UI strings and at least two sample locales.

The demo should make one business question easy to evaluate:

> Can an operations user understand an ERP exception faster and make a confident decision with AI assistance?

Keep all AI responses deterministic and locally defined. Include visible “Demo data” and “Simulated AI” labels.

Out of scope: authentication, APIs, backend services, real model calls, credentials, production data, integrations, persistence beyond the browser session, and actual approvals or transactions.

Success criteria:

- A user can open the demo and reach the exception inbox immediately.
- They can inspect one exception, understand why it was flagged, and complete a decision in under two minutes.
- The prototype works comfortably on desktop and mobile widths.
- A reviewer can distinguish mock data, simulated reasoning, and user actions from real ERP behavior.
