Simplify it with a mobile-first, progressive-disclosure layout:

1. **Header**
   - Name and party type
   - Status/segment badge
   - One primary action

2. **At-a-glance summary**
   - Contact method
   - Account/credit status
   - Outstanding balance
   - Next important action

3. **Expandable sections**
   - Contact & addresses
   - Financials: tax IDs, credit, invoices
   - Recent activity
   - Relationship notes
   - Segmentation

4. **Audit & compliance**
   - Keep available in a clearly labeled, collapsed “Compliance and audit” section.
   - Show a brief “last updated” indicator without exposing every metadata field upfront.

Use tabs or accordions, prioritize the most actionable information, and move secondary details behind “View more.” This reduces scanning effort without permanently removing any information.
