Choose hybrid BM25+dense retrieval as the default.

The workload has a substantial paraphrase component, where dense retrieval should improve coverage, while BM25 preserves strong matching for exact policy/article IDs and error codes. Use BM25 as a required lexical component, with dense retrieval contributing candidates or reranking.

Trade-offs:

- Better expected paraphrase recall and broader query coverage.
- Higher indexing, operational, tuning, and latency costs.
- More complexity and potential relevance regressions if weighting is poorly calibrated.
- BM25 alone remains attractive if latency and simplicity dominate, especially for the 60% exact-match traffic.

This is an engineering default, not a claim of measured performance. Validate it with a labeled benchmark and establish a latency SLO before approving production rollout.
