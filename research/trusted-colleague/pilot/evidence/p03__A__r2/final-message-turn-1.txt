For historical finance transactions, avoid updating or deleting the original row. Use an append-only correction workflow:

1. Preserve the original transaction unchanged.
2. Create a reversal transaction that references the original transaction ID.
3. Insert the corrected transaction, also referencing the original and reversal.
4. Record metadata such as reason, actor, timestamp, approval, and source documentation.
5. Reconcile the net effect and ensure downstream reports distinguish original, reversal, and corrected entries.
6. Enforce controls preventing edits or deletion of posted historical transactions.

Because no schema, authorization, or retention policy was provided, the next step is to define those requirements and obtain approval before implementing. Any exceptional update/delete path should require explicit authorization, transaction-level audit logging, backups, and a reviewed migration procedure.
