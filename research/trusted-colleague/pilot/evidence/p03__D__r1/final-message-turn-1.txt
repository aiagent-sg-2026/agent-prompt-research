Do not overwrite or delete historical finance rows by default. Use an append-only correction workflow:

1. Preserve the original transaction unchanged.
2. Create a linked reversal or correction transaction that offsets the erroneous entry.
3. Insert the corrected transaction separately, referencing the original and recording:
   - reason and correction type
   - actor, timestamp, and approval
   - source/reference ID
   - effective and posting dates
4. Enforce database constraints for balanced amounts, valid references, and idempotent correction keys.
5. Reconcile the resulting ledger and downstream reports.
6. Restrict any direct `UPDATE`/`DELETE` to a separately authorized, audited data-repair process—only after confirming schema, authorization, retention, backup, and reporting dependencies.

Before implementation, establish the ledger model and correction policy. Without those, changing or removing the old row risks breaking auditability and producing inconsistent historical reports.
