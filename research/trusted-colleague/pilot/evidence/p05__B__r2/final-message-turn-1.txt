Build a single responsive “AI Operations Copilot” slice focused on one high-value ERP workflow: turning an exception into an actionable decision.

The demo should include:

- A dashboard showing a few static operational exceptions, such as delayed orders, low stock, or overdue invoices.
- A selected exception detail view with business context, impact, and recommended next actions.
- A conversational AI panel using scripted, predefined responses—no API calls or backend.
- Action previews such as “Draft supplier message” or “Create replenishment request,” clearly marked as simulated and requiring confirmation.
- Lightweight success indicators: time saved, risk reduced, and estimated business impact.

Keep all data local in structured mock objects so the experience can be tested entirely in the browser. Make the layout responsive from the start, with touch-friendly interactions and an app-shell structure suitable for later PWA support. Add a basic internationalization layer for visible labels and messages, even if the first demo ships in one language.

The validation question should be:

“Can an operations user understand an ERP exception, trust the AI recommendation, and know what to do next within one minute?”

Avoid authentication, persistence, real notifications, credentials, integrations, or claims that actions actually affect ERP systems.
