Choose hybrid BM25 + dense retrieval as the default.

The workload is mixed: BM25 is strongest for the 60% exact identifiers and error codes, while dense retrieval should improve coverage for the 40% paraphrased questions. Use BM25 as a required retrieval path and combine or rerank its results with dense matches, preserving exact-match priority.

Trade-offs:

- Better expected paraphrase recall and broader coverage.
- Higher indexing, operational, tuning, and latency costs.
- More system complexity than BM25 alone.
- BM25 remains simpler and faster, but risks missing relevant paraphrased questions.

This is an engineering default, not a claim about measured recall or latency; without an SLO or labeled benchmark, validate the choice before production rollout.
