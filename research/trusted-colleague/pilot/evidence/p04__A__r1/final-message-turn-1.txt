Simplify it into a focused, mobile-first summary:

- Header: name, party type, status, and one primary action.
- Key facts: phone/email, relationship owner, segment, and credit status.
- Financial snapshot: open invoice total and overdue count.
- Recent activity: show the latest 2–3 items, with “View all.”
- Addresses, tax identifiers, relationship notes, and audit metadata: move into collapsible sections or a secondary “Details” view.
- Actions: limit to the most common 2–3 actions; place the rest under “More.”

Use progressive disclosure so the modal answers “Who is this, what needs attention, and what can I do?” immediately, while retaining all compliance and audit information behind clearly labeled sections.
