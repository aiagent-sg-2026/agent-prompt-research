Simplify it with a mobile-first, progressive-disclosure layout:

1. **Header**
   - Name, party type, status, and one primary action.
   - Keep contact details immediately accessible.

2. **Overview**
   - Relationship health or credit status
   - Open invoice total and count
   - Most recent activity

3. **Expandable sections**
   - Contact & addresses
   - Financial details: tax identifiers, credit status, invoices
   - Activity
   - Relationship notes
   - Segmentation
   - Actions

4. **Audit & compliance**
   - Keep audit metadata and tax identifiers available in a clearly labeled “Compliance & audit” section, collapsed by default.

Use summary cards, short labels, and a single-column layout. Avoid showing every field at once; reveal details only when requested. This is reversible because all existing information remains available, just reorganized behind expandable sections.
