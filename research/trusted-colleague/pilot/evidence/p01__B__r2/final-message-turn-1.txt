The likely cause is row multiplication: each invoice joins to multiple `invoice_lines` and multiple `customer_tags`, producing a many-to-many combination. For example, 3 lines × 2 tags can yield 6 rows for one invoice.

`SELECT DISTINCT` may hide identical projected values, but it does not reliably restore one row per invoice and can mask incorrect totals or differing line/tag data.
