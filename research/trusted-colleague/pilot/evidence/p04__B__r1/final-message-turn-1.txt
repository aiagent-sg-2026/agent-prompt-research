Simplify it around the most common decision: “Who is this, and what needs attention?”

Proposed modal structure:

1. Header
   - Name
   - Customer/party type
   - Status or risk badge
   - Close button

2. Key summary
   - Primary contact
   - Location
   - Credit status
   - Outstanding balance or open invoices

3. Next actions
   - Contact
   - View invoices
   - Edit details
   - More actions

4. Activity
   - Recent activity and relationship notes in a compact, collapsible section

5. Details
   - Addresses, tax identifiers, segmentation, and audit metadata behind “View full details”

Keep compliance and audit information available, but move it out of the default mobile view. Use progressive disclosure, short labels, and a sticky action area so the modal remains useful without overwhelming users.

Assumption: the default view should prioritize servicing and collections workflows; role-specific priorities may require adjusting the summary fields.
